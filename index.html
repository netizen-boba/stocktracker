Tracked stocks

US stocks, ETFs and mutual funds (try SWPPX) — funds are marked Fund and price once a day at NAV. Non-US listings work too (ASML finds the Amsterdam line) and are marked EUR→USD: their whole history is converted to dollars at each day's ECB rate, so the numbers include the currency move. Track as many as you like — the chart holds 8 at a time so the colours stay distinguishable, and every stock still appears in the metrics table. Click tiles to swap what's charted. Benchmark: SPY

Alert rules

Each rule reads as: notify me if [which stock] [moves] [X]% over [N] days. Trading days counts market sessions — a 3-day window is always 3 sessions. Calendar days counts dates, so a 3-day window checked on a Monday looks back to Friday. Use trading days for short windows, calendar for “over a month”.
Changes save in this browser instantly. To let the twice-daily Claude monitor use them, save config.json back into your Stock tracker folder — or just tell Claude the change in chat. Load config.json goes the other way: it pulls in a file Claude has edited, replacing the watchlist and rules held in this browser.
Prices and fundamentals from stockanalysis.com, fetched live by your browser. Daily closes; the latest point is today's quote and updates through the session. Mutual funds are the exception — one NAV a day, published after the close, and no intraday data. Non-US listings are converted to USD at the ECB's daily reference rate (via frankfurter.dev), so their returns include the currency move. Signals are arithmetic on price history (RSI, moving averages, drawdown, relative strength) — they describe what the chart has done, not what it will do, and are not financial advice.
